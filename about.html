About this guide

ZionNationalPark.org is an independent visitor guide. It is not operated by, endorsed by, or affiliated with the National Park Service, the U.S. Department of the Interior, or any other government agency.

Official information

Zion National Park is a unit of the National Park Service. Alerts, permits, fees, shuttle schedules, and maps come from nps.gov/zion. We link there whenever a rule matters.

What this site does

We explain the trails, seasons, and logistics that shape a trip to Zion, and we help visitors find lodging, guides, gear, and transportation. When you send a request through the trip planning form, it may be shared with providers who can help.

Accuracy

Information was last reviewed in October 2026. Park conditions, trail access, permit systems, and fees change, so treat numbers here as approximate and confirm them with the National Park Service before you travel.
