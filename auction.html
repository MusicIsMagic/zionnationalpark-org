    What the buyer gets

    • The name: ZionNationalPark.org, an exact-match name for visitors searching for Zion National Park.
    • A finished website: Home, Hikes, When to visit, Stay and tours, Plan your trip, About, Privacy policy, Thank-you and 404 pages. It includes trail guides, a season-by-season planner, lodging and tour guidance, all written for this site.

    It is best suited to tour operators, lodging, outfitters, shuttle and gear-rental businesses, and anyone who earns from Zion visitors.

    How the auction works

    1. The opening bid sets the clock.The first confirmed bid at or above the minimum starts a 30-day countdown.
    2. Raise the bid.Each new bid must beat the high bid by at least $100.
    3. No last-second wins.A bid in the final 72 hours moves the close to 72 hours after that bid, so everyone has time to respond.
    4. Confirm by email.A bid counts only after you click the link we email you.
    5. The owner does not bid.Every bid on this page comes from an outside bidder.

    Terms

    A confirmed bid is binding on the bidder. The highest confirmed bid when the auction closes wins. The winner opens an Escrow.com transaction within 7 days of closing and pays through it. The buyer pays the escrow fee. If the winner does not complete payment, the owner may offer the property to the next-highest bidder. The property transfers when Escrow.com confirms payment. Images on this site are illustrative and may be computer-generated. This is an independent visitor guide and is not affiliated with the National Park Service or any government agency, and no traffic, revenue, or search-ranking figures are claimed or implied.

    Right to remove bids and cancel. Until a bidder has funded an Escrow.com transaction for this property, the owner may, at the owner's sole discretion and at any time, remove any bid or cancel the auction, for any reason, including if a bidder cannot be reached, does not respond, or does not open or fund escrow. A removed bid or a cancelled auction releases the bidder, who then owes nothing. Once a buyer has funded an Escrow.com transaction for this property, the owner will not cancel the sale or remove that bid, and the sale will be completed under the Escrow.com terms. Removed bids are counted on this page.
